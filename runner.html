PSA Sync

Reads the world rankings and the entry list of every upcoming tournament from SecurePSA, using the login already open in this browser, and sends them to your planner. Nothing is stored here but the token below.

Setup

Planner → Settings → Entry lists → Copy. It identifies your planner account; it is not your PSA password.
Change this only if your planner lives somewhere else.

Sync

If something goes wrong

  1. Open secure.psasquashtour.com in another tab and check you are signed in, then try again.
  2. A few tournaments failing is normal — some have no entry list yet. The run continues past them.
  3. Rankings come first, then the tournaments. A full run takes two to three minutes.
  4. If every tournament fails, the token is usually wrong: copy it again from the planner's Settings tab.
